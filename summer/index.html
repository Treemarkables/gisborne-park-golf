Questions, before you ring

How do I join?

Phone 06 867 9849, email info@gisbornepark.co.nz, or call into the clubhouse at Cochrane Street, Elgin, Gisborne 4010 (open 8am to 4pm). There is no online checkout.

What does summer membership cover?

Eighteen holes — the full course — through the summer season, for $350. It is a seasonal club membership, not a casual green fee.

Do I need to book a tee time?

No. The Park is a relaxed club with no booking system. Just turn up and play — or ring / email first if you prefer a yarn before you arrive.

When does summer membership run?

Dates are set each season by the club — they are not listed as fixed calendar days on this site. Phone the clubhouse on 06 867 9849 and they will confirm this summer’s start and finish. Twilight golf is on summer evenings.

What if I want to play all year?

Full-year options live on Membership & Fees — first-year member $700, 18-hole full $800, nine-hole & social $600, all GST inclusive.
