A club small enough to know your name.

Mature trees, honest golf, five minutes from town beside Gisborne Airport. Straight hitters prosper. Come for the golf, stay for the company — the clubhouse runs 8am to 4pm, with twilight nine-holes on summer evenings.

  • Tree-lined parkland on the flat — winter golf here is actual golf
  • Clubhouse privileges, including the simulator on wet days
  • Twilight golf on summer evenings, then a barbecue on the deck
  • Casual visitors welcome every other day of the year too

How to join

No forms on this website, on purpose. Ring the clubhouse or call in (clubhouse open 8am to 4pm), have a yarn, and we’ll sort the rest over a cuppa.

Questions, before you ring

When does summer membership run?

Dates are set each season by the club — they are not listed as fixed calendar days on this site. Phone the clubhouse on 06 867 9849 and they will confirm this summer’s start and finish. Twilight golf is on summer evenings.

Who is summer membership for?

Golfers who play in the warmer months, people trying the Park before a full-year sub, and anyone who wants a proper membership without paying for the whole year. Nine-hole suits shorter rounds and twilight; eighteen-hole is for the full course.

What’s the difference between $250 and $400?

Summer 9-hole is $250 including GST and covers nine-hole play. Summer 18-hole is $400 including GST and covers the full eighteen. Both are seasonal memberships, not casual green fees.

How do I join?

Phone 06 867 9849, email info@gisbornepark.co.nz, or call into the clubhouse at Cochrane Street, Elgin, Gisborne 4010 (open 8am to 4pm). There is no online checkout.

What if I want to play all year?

Full-year options live on Membership & Fees — first-year member $700, 18-hole full $800, nine-hole & social $600, all GST inclusive.
